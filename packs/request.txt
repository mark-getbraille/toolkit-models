# its limit (for a pack that stopped at the time limit; it can be asked for again and again). Without the plus the
# pack is built again from the start. A top-up that fails or finds nothing new leaves the published pack as it was.
# A key with a question mark (loc_highsmith?) is a needs-only run: only the pictures dropped for a missing or poor
# description are collected and published in the release needs-description; the pack itself is not changed.
