HOW TO PREPARE PICTURES FOR TRAINING (the Dataset Preparation program)

Dataset Preparation gets your own pictures and videos ready for training, so the person who reviews them starts
from a good draft instead of an empty box. You find it next to the training program, as Dataset-Preparation.exe,
in the same place you got Picture-Model-Training.exe.

Use it when no training is running. The training and the drafts both use the graphics card, and they cannot share
it. Your first training must have finished, or be stopped, before you write drafts.

WHAT IT DOES, STEP BY STEP

1. Choose a workspace.
   A workspace is an ordinary folder of pictures, with a description for each picture in a text file of the same
   name. It is the same kind of workspace as in Describe pictures, in the training program. Choose an empty folder
   to make a new one (Browse for the workspace folder, Alt+B).
   The Training kit folder (Alt+F) is where the training kit is, normally C:\Training\Picture Model Training Kit.
   Its tools and models are used, so nothing is installed twice.

2. Put pictures and videos in.
   - Add pictures (Alt+P) and Add a picture folder (Alt+O) copy pictures in. A picture already there is not added
     twice, and very big pictures are made smaller.
   - Take stills from videos (Alt+V) takes good, sharp still pictures out of videos. It leaves out dark, blurry and
     repeated ones, gives each still what you said at that moment as its description, and can blur faces. It also
     removes burned-in marks: a camcorder's date and time stamp, a TV station's logo or a fixed news ticker. Choose
     "Crop them off" or "Fill them in" under "Date stamps and logos".

3. Clean up (Alt+C).
   - Set aside dark, blurry and repeated pictures (Alt+E). Nothing is deleted. Set-aside pictures get the status
     "skip", and you can see them in Describe pictures with Filter, "Set aside or skipped". A picture that already
     has a description is never set aside. Of two pictures that look the same, the sharper one is kept.
   - Blur the faces of people (Alt+U).
   - Date stamps and logos in the pictures (Alt+G): for photos, as for videos. A mark is found by what stays the
     same over many pictures of the same size, such as all the photos from one camera, or the frames of one news
     programme. It needs about a dozen pictures of the same size.
   Whenever a picture is changed, its original is kept in the hidden folder ".originals" inside the workspace.

4. Write the drafts (Alt+A).
   Several picture models each write a draft description of every new picture that has no description yet:
   - Qwen3-VL 8B (Alt+Q): long, plain descriptions. It does not refuse pictures of bodies or injuries.
   - InternVL 3.5 8B (Alt+I): a second opinion from a different family of model. It learned from different
     pictures, so its mistakes are different ones.
   - Florence-2 large (Alt+R): small and quick. It gives a detailed caption and reads the text in the picture.
   - Your own trained describer (Alt+Y): the model your training made. It can be ticked once a training has
     finished.
   Combine the drafts (Alt+M): Qwen3-VL looks at each picture again, together with all the drafts, and writes one
   starting description. It keeps what is right, corrects what is wrong and adds what was missed.
   The first time, each model is downloaded once (Qwen3-VL and InternVL about 17 GB each, Florence-2 about
   1.5 GB) and kept in the kit's work folder. Each model is loaded once and describes every picture before the next
   one starts. Expect roughly 10 to 30 seconds per picture for each large model.
   Clean up, then write the drafts (Alt+N) does both steps, one after the other. It is good to leave running
   overnight.
   Stop (Alt+S) stops the work. Start it again later: pictures that already have a draft are not done again.

5. Review and correct (Alt+W).
   This opens Describe pictures on the workspace. For a picture without a description:
   - The Description box already holds the computer's starting draft. When the picture is read out, it is called
     "Computer's draft, not checked yet". Check it against the picture, correct it, then press Save and next
     (Alt+S) or Ctrl+S.
   - "Drafts by each picture model" (Alt+J) has each model's own draft, and says whether they agree well, partly
     agree or disagree a lot.
   - The Filter "Not described: drafts that disagree first" lists first the pictures that need the most care.
   - A draft is only a draft. Moving to another picture without saving leaves it unsaved, and nothing is trained on
     until a person saves a description. A saved description that started from a draft is marked as such.
   You can also export the pictures as a package for a sighted friend (Export a package). The drafts travel with
   the pictures, so your friend starts from them too.

6. Train on it.
   In the training program, in Describe pictures, press "Use this workspace for training". Then start a training,
   or a follow-up training. Only described and checked pictures are used.

7. Pictures that need a description (Alt+T).
   "Build a review pack" opens its own window: it downloads the pictures the training packs set aside because their
   description was missing or poor, reads each picture's web page and has two models write drafts. See "HOW TO
   DESCRIBE PICTURES THAT NEED A DESCRIPTION.txt" (F1 in that window).

KEYS
   Escape closes the program (it asks first while work is running). F1 opens this guide. Every button and check box
   has an Alt letter, and its tooltip names it. Enter presses only the button you are on.
   Letters: K workspace folder, B browse for the workspace, F kit folder, D browse for the kit, P add pictures,
   O add a picture folder, V stills from videos, E set aside, U blur faces, G date stamps and logos, C clean up,
   Q Qwen3-VL, I InternVL, R Florence-2, Y your own describer, M combine, A write the drafts, N clean up then drafts,
   W review, S stop, T build a review pack, H help, L close.
   The Status field, the Progress bar and "What was done" show what is happening. The important lines are spoken.
   Everything is also written into the "Picture Model Training logs" folder on the desktop.

GOOD TO KNOW
   - Everything runs on this computer. Pictures are never sent anywhere. Only the models are downloaded, once (and,
     for a review pack, the pictures that need a description and their web pages).
   - The licences: Qwen3-VL and InternVL are Apache-2.0, and Florence-2 is MIT. A draft is a starting point, not a
     description: the descriptions the training learns from are the ones people saved.
   - Pictures of children: the same rules as everywhere in the kit. Never describe anyone who may be under 18 in a
     sexual way. Every model is told this.
