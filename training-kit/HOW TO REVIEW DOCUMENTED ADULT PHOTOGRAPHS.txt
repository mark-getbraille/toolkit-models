HOW TO REVIEW DOCUMENTED ADULT PHOTOGRAPHS

What this is
These are photographs of nudity or of sex between adults from institution catalogues (the Library of Congress and
the Wellcome Collection). A photograph is only ever downloaded when the institution's own record documents that
every person in it is an adult: either the record says so ("adult models", "consenting adults"), or every person it
names has a birth year that shows they were 18 or over when the photo was taken. Anonymous photographs, and any
record with a word about a child or a young person, are never downloaded or shown.

Such photographs are approved automatically. This window is for an optional second look.

Getting them (on your own computer only; nothing is published)
  python training-kit\kit\adult_photo_review.py search "B:\Picture Library\Review\Documented adult photographs"
Then open the window:
  python training-kit\wizard\adult_photo_window.py "B:\Picture Library\Review\Documented adult photographs"

The window
Type your name once. Each picture is shown with its title, institution, the reason it was approved, its rights and
the catalogue's words.
  Approve (Alt+A, or Y): every person is clearly an adult. Your name and the time are written down.
  Reject (Alt+R, or N): the picture is deleted and never fetched again.
  Unsure (Alt+S): counts as Reject.
  Open the catalogue page (Alt+O): the institution's own page in your browser.
  Escape closes. F1 opens this guide.
When in doubt, reject.

Training
The approved pictures are in the "approved" folder, in describing workspaces. Training uses them only when you
choose them as review folders and make the matching choices (adult sexual content needs art with nudity; pictures
whose rights are not open need the personal-use choice). A training with them is for personal use only and is never
published. They are never used for picture-making.
